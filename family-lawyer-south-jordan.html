List of Pre-World Cup Friendlies to be Played in the United States

The World Cup is just over one month away. A total of 104 games will be played at this World Cup, the most ever following the expansion of the tournament to 48 teams. Ten of those games will be played in Canada with another ten to be played in Mexico. The remaining 84 games, however, will be played in the United States, including everything from the quarterfinals onward. The hope among soccer fans in the US is that this World Cup will provide a substantial and sustained boost to the growth of the game in this country. Only eleven US cities will be hosting games, but it’s not just the games themselves that can grow the fan base and love of the sport. Many US cities that don’t have games are home to base camps for some of the World Cup teams (Germany in Winston-Salem and Spain in Chattanooga, for example). Still others will be hosting fan fests and watch parties. And finally, a number of cities will be hosting pre-World Cup friendlies, which aren’t World Cup games obviously but still provide an opportunity for people to go watch high-level international soccer. Some of the games figure to draw huge crowds, like Mexico at the Rose Bowl and Brazil in Cleveland. The USMNT is the host of the tournament and, one would think, be set for large crowds for their two friendlies against Senegal in Charlotte and Germany in Chicago. But their last two friendlies in March were very ugly losse s, despite being played in front of huge crowds in Atlanta. Some interesting venues have been chosen for these pre-World Cup friendlies. Toyota Field in San Antonio is the home of USL Championship side San Antonio FC and will host Saudi Arabia vs. Senegal. Inter Miami’s new home (Nu Stadium) gets to see Peru vs. Haiti while their old home (Chase Stadium) gets two games: New Zealand vs. Haiti and Venezuela vs. Turkey. Perhaps the most interesting venues are the two college football stadiums that Argentina has picked: Kyle Field, home of the Texas A&M Aggies, in College Station, TX and Jordan Hare Stadium, home of the Auburn Tigers, in Auburn, AL. Kyle Field has hosted international soccer before, with Mexico taking on Brazil prior to the 2024 Copa America, but this will be the first ever soccer game at Jordan Hare Stadium. And given that one of the biggest talking points of this World Cup is, unfortunately, the price of tickets, getting to go to a pre-tournament friendly may be the best and most affordable option for a new soccer fan looking to experience high-level international soccer up close.
